Our Mission

We champion the rights of persons with disabilities, enabling full and equal participation in Mongolian society. Founded 2019 in Orkhon · Expanded 2025 to Darkhan-Uul.

🤝

Counselling & InformationLegal and social service guidance. Psychological support to 154+ individuals.

📢
